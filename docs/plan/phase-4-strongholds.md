# Phase 4 — Strongholds

**Milestone (owner playtest):** with `/locate structure mallowpiece:pirate_den` and `/locate structure mallowpiece:rev_hideout`, both generate in a fresh world. They look like places, have Revolutionary and pirate NPCs, and hold T3 loot.

Fixes W3 (same structures every time) and W4 (faction fairness). Nobody builds by hand, so **everything is generated by code**. A human can polish it later in-game and re-save with structure blocks.

## 4.1 Structure generator — `tools/structgen` (L)
**Goal:** a Python tool that writes vanilla structure templates (`.nbt`), jigsaw template pools and worldgen JSON from a readable building description. It's used for every structure in Phases 4–6.

**Design**
- Python 3.10+, no deps beyond the stdlib. Write an NBT **writer** next to the existing reader (`tools/analysis/nbt.py`): gzip, TAG_Compound root, `DataVersion` 3465 (1.20.1), `size`, `palette`, `blocks` (`pos`, `state`, optional `nbt`), `entities: []`.
- Building blocks (`tools/structgen/lib/`):
  - `Canvas(w, h, d)`: set blocks with states (`"minecraft:spruce_stairs[facing=north,half=bottom]"`), fill, hollow box, line, replace.
  - Shapes: `room(x,y,z,w,h,d, wall, floor, ceiling, door_sides, windows)`, `gable_roof`, `hip_roof`, `tower(radius, height, cap)`, `palisade(path, height)`, `stairs_between(a, b)`, `ladder_shaft`, `tunnel(path, width, height, support_every=4)`.
  - Decor: `decorate(room, kit)`, where kits are lists of props with placement rules (`against_wall`, `corner`, `centre`): beds, tables (fence + pressure plate), barrels, chests, lanterns, banners, cartography/smithing tables, cannons (MMnM's cannon entity if it can be pre-placed; otherwise a dispenser + iron block "cannon" prop), weapon racks (armor stands are entities → skip; use item frames only if supported).
  - Weathering: random replace (10–20%) of a palette block with its mossy/cracked variants, so surfaces aren't flat.
  - **Markers:** `chest(pos, loot_table)` writes `{LootTable: "..."}` block-entity NBT; `mob(pos, pool)` writes a `minecraft:jigsaw` (`name`/`target` `mineminenomi:mob`, `pool` = our mob pool) matching MMnM's mob-marker convention; `connector(pos, facing, name, target_pool)` for jigsaw links.
  - Mob templates: generate our own 2×1×1 mob pieces (jigsaw + `mineminenomi:custom_spawner` with `entityToSpawn`, `spawnLimit`, `playerDistance: 100`), the same format MMnM uses (see API notes).
- Output goes to `src/main/resources/data/mallowpiece/structures/...`, `worldgen/template_pool/...`, `worldgen/structure/...`, `worldgen/structure_set/...`, and `tags/worldgen/biome/has_structure/...`. These are ours and are committed.
- `python3 tools/structgen/build.py pirate_den` regenerates one structure; `build.py all` regenerates everything. Output must be deterministic (seeded RNG), so diffs stay readable.
- **Preview:** `tools/structgen/preview.py <nbt>` renders a top-down PNG and a 3/4 isometric PNG (Pillow allowed for preview only) with a simple colour per block family, for checking layouts without launching the game.
- **Reference:** read MMnM's own templates (`.reference/.../jar/data/mineminenomi/structures/marine/large_base/*.nbt`) with the reader to match their scale and style. Don't copy them.

**Acceptance:** `build.py test_hut` produces a 9×7×9 hut with a chest and one mob marker. `/place template mallowpiece:test_hut` in game shows the hut, the chest has loot, and the mob spawns.

## 4.2 Revolutionary NPC set (M)
**Design**

| Entity id | Built on | Doriki | Look (derived texture recipe) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mallowpiece:rev_grunt` | `GruntEntity` | as grunt | marine grunt skin → dark olive/black coat, tan trousers, hood |
| `mallowpiece:rev_sniper` | `SniperEntity` | as sniper | sniper skin → same palette, goggles |
| `mallowpiece:rev_brute` | `BruteEntity` | as brute | brute skin → same palette, bandaged arms |
| `mallowpiece:rev_captain` | `CaptainEntity` | as captain | captain skin → long black coat, red armband |
| `mallowpiece:rev_commander` | (2.4 elite) | 7,000–9,000 | captain skin → black coat, gold trim, scarf |

- Faction: `ModFactions.REVOLUTIONARY_ARMY` via `setFactionInit`. MMnM's revolutionary hurt check already stops revs from hurting revs and civilians, so they're hostile to marines, WG, pirates and bandits, and friendly to revolutionary players.
- Loot: same as the marine equivalent (`mineminenomi:entities/marine_*`), via our own loot tables that reference theirs (`"type": "minecraft:loot_table"` entries).
- Textures: derive recipes (0.4). If a derived look is bad, draw an original 64×64 skin in `src/main/resources` instead.

**Acceptance:** summon each one; marine NPCs fight them, civilians are left alone, and a revolutionary player can walk past.

## 4.4 Pirate Den (L)
**Concept:** a rival pirate crew's land fortress. Pirate NPCs aren't in your crew, so they're hostile to *every* player, pirates included (MMnM: pirates can hurt pirates unless they're crewmates). It's fair for all factions.

**Layout** (about 56×24×56, jigsaw pieces; `G` gate, `T` tower, `H` hall, `Q` captain's quarters, `B` bunkhouse, `C` cannon platform, `A` armory):
```
 C . . . . . . . . . . . . C
 . T . . B B B . . A A . T .
 . . . . B B B . . A A . . .
 . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
 G . . . . H H H H . . Q Q .
 . . . . . H H H H . . Q Q .
 . . . . . H H H H . . Q Q .
 . T . . B B B . . . . . T .
 C . . . . . . . . . . . . C
```
- **Palisade:** spruce logs 5–6 high, with sharpened tops (spruce fence). Walkway on the inside with ladders. Weathered.
- **Gate:** double door frame of stripped logs, with banner slots for a Jolly Roger (plain black banner).
- **Grog Hall** (`H`, 14×8×10): long tables, barrels, kegs (barrels on their side), lanterns, a bar. 4 grunts and 1 brute. Food chest (`mineminenomi:chests/tavern/food`).
- **Captain's Quarters** (`Q`, 3-storey tower, 9×16×9): ground = map room; 2nd = bedroom; top = treasure room with the **captain chest `mallowpiece:chests/pirate_den/captain` (T3)**, the pirate captain, and 50% a Notorious Officer elite.
- **Bunkhouses** (`B` ×2, 9×6×7): beds, chests (`chests/pirate/medium_ship/supplies`), 3 grunts each.
- **Armory** (`A`, 8×6×8): T2 chest `mallowpiece:chests/pirate_den/armory` (weapons, bullets, 15% iron box), 2 brutes.
- **Lookout towers** (`T` ×4, 5×12×5): one pirate sniper each.
- **Cannon platforms** (`C` ×4): cannon props facing outwards.
- **Hidden stash:** a trapdoor under the hall's bar leads to a 5×3×5 cellar with a T2 chest. 35% chance the piece generates.
- **Variation (jigsaw):** 2 hall variants, 2 quarters variants, bunkhouses can be swapped for a smithy or a prison (cells with 1–2 civilians, `mineminenomi:skypiean_civilian` or vanilla villager: rescuing is flavour only).
- **Placement:** biomes = beach, plains, savanna, forest, birch forest, taiga, jungle edge (`#mallowpiece:has_structure/pirate_den`). Structure set spacing 56 / separation 28 (a little more common than the marine large base). Terrain: `"terrain_adaptation": "beard_box"`, start height from the heightmap.
- Mob counts total: ~10 grunts, 3 brutes, 4 snipers, 1 captain, 0–1 elite.

**Acceptance:** generates in 5 test locations (TESTLOG with screenshots); clear it as a pirate with no crew; NPCs are hostile; the captain chest uses the T3 table.

## 4.5 Revolutionary Hideout (L)
**Concept:** hidden underground, like MMnM's bandit caves but bigger and themed. The surface shows only a ruined farmhouse.

**Layout**
```
Surface:   [ruined farmhouse 11×7×9]  — a ladder shaft in the cellar goes down 18 blocks
Level -1:  ┌────────── Hub (15×7×15): map table, banners, 3 grunts, 1 sniper on a balcony ──────────┐
           │  ├─ Barracks (12×5×9): bunks, supplies chest, 4 grunts                                  │
           │  ├─ Armory (9×5×9): T2 chest `mallowpiece:chests/rev_hideout/armory`, 2 brutes          │
           │  ├─ Printing room (9×5×7): paper, lecterns, cartography table, 1 grunt                  │
           │  ├─ Prison (11×5×9): cells, 1–2 captured marines (marine grunts, hostile when freed)    │
           │  └─ War Room (13×6×11): big table, maps, CAPTAIN CHEST (T3) `.../war_room`,             │
           │     rev captain + 50% Revolutionary Commander                                           │
           └── Escape tunnel (30–50 blocks, 3×3) to a hidden exit (trapdoor in a bush) ─────────────┘
```
- Tunnels between rooms: 3 wide × 3 high, stone bricks plus spruce supports every 4 blocks, lanterns every 8, jigsaw-connected so the layout varies (hub with 4–6 connectors; rooms in random order).
- **Placement:** most overworld land biomes except ocean and river (`#mallowpiece:has_structure/rev_hideout`). Spacing 60 / separation 30. Start height: surface for the farmhouse; the underground pieces are placed relative to it via the shaft.
- **Fairness:** revolutionary players are friendly with these NPCs, so the war room chest gets the same rank lock as marine bases (1.6), using `RevolutionaryRank` (read its loyalty thresholds in the reference): War Room needs the equivalent of Captain. Stealing makes you rogue, and rev NPCs hunt you.

**Acceptance:** generates in 5 test locations; walk in from the farmhouse to the war room; a marine clears it; a revolutionary with low rank gets the theft warning.

## 4.6 Faction fairness: enemy strongholds per faction (S)
After 4.4 and 4.5, every faction has at least one T3 stronghold to raid:

| Player faction | Enemy T3 strongholds |
|---|---|
| Pirate | Marine large base, battleship, Pirate Den (rival crew), Rev Hideout* |
| Marine / Bounty Hunter | Pirate Den, Rev Hideout, pirate large ship, bandit fort (T2) |
| Revolutionary | Marine large base, battleship, Pirate Den |

\*Rev NPCs only attack pirates who attack them first? **Decision: no.** Rev NPCs attack pirates on sight (MMnM's rev hurt check allows it), so the hideout is a valid target for pirates.

- Update the breakthrough data (2.3) so "enemy structure" uses this table (`#mallowpiece:enemy_strongholds/<faction>` structure tags).
- Rank-locked "own faction" chests: marine (1.6) and revolutionary (4.5). Pirates have no own-faction stronghold that's friendly, so nothing to lock.
